# Privacy Policy / গোপনীয়তা নীতি

**Quran Navigation** stores your bookmarks, reading position, hifz records, language, theme and selected reciter **only on your device**. We do not run servers that collect your data, and the app has no accounts, advertising or analytics.

**Audio:** recitation audio is streamed from everyayah.com. When you play audio, that site receives your IP address and the requested ayah, as any website does.

**Backups:** when you export a hifz backup, the file is saved to your device. You control where it is shared.

**Children:** the app may be used by students who are minors. Parents and teachers should review hifz records together. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
